THE REAL JACK HINES

John Joseph “Jack” Hines III

November 25, 1947 – April 4, 2025

JACK is named in honor of a respected radio communications leader, mentor, businessman, and friend whose career spanned more than fifty years. Jack combined deep industry knowledge and determined leadership with humor, kindness, and a genuine commitment to the people he served.

A lifetime in radio communications

Born in Philadelphia, Jack built a career across RCA, Syntonic, OCI, ORBACOM, Pinnacle Wireless–Mindshare, and CSS–Mindshare. When CSS acquired the Mindshare product line in 2013, Jack became its General Manager and helped guide its growth into a respected presence in the public-safety dispatch market.

He retired from CSS–Mindshare in December 2024 after more than five decades in the industry. His professional legacy was not limited to selling equipment. He listened to customers, understood their operational problems, stood behind the product, and turned business relationships into lasting friendships.

How people remember him

  • A technically knowledgeable and decisive business leader
  • A patient mentor who helped others build their careers
  • A customer advocate focused on practical solutions
  • A devoted husband, father, and proud family man
  • A natural storyteller with unforgettable humor
  • A caring friend who treated people with respect

Why the technical assistant carries his name

The JACK assistant is designed to preserve technical knowledge, give direct and honest answers, make complicated systems understandable, and help the people responsible for mission-critical communications. Those goals reflect the customer-first qualities that Jack brought to the radio communications industry.
